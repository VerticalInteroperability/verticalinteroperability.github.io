## About this site

**Subject:** vertical interoperability: research data keeping its meaning across the research lifecycle.
**Group:** VECTOR, a working group formed at a 2026 workshop on vertical interoperability.
**Status:** early. The site is a holding page; publications, events and resources will be added as the group agrees to
share them.
**Hosting:** the site and GitHub organization are hosted by Axius SDC, Inc. (https://axius-sdc.com) as a contribution
to the group. Axius doesn't speak for VECTOR, and VECTOR doesn't endorse Axius or any other company's products.

## How to describe vertical interoperability

- It's about time and the research lifecycle, not only moving data between systems.
- Its test is reuse: can someone who wasn't involved in the original work understand the data's context and reuse it
  meaningfully?
- The site gives a general explanation. **The group's formal definition isn't published yet.** Don't present the
  site's wording as VECTOR's official definition, and don't infer one.

## Please don't

- **Attribute views to individual members.** The group's discussions are not attributed to individuals, and the site
  names no members.
- **Describe unpublished work, grant plans or meeting content** as if it came from this site. It doesn't.
- **Present VECTOR as endorsing any tool, standard or vendor,** including the site's host.
- **Confuse VECTOR with other projects** that use the word "vector" (vector databases, vector graphics, disease
  vectors).
